THE STORY

Ananas Joe

Ananas Joe lives on a tiny tropical island where summer never really ends. He has a very simple philosophy:

Paddle. Chill. Nap. Coffee. Repeat.

Every morning, Joe takes his board and heads into the Endless Sea, searching for the perfect summer wave. Nobody knows if the wave actually exists. Joe doesn’t really care. He just likes the ride.

The Endless Sea

The Endless Sea changes every time Joe goes out. Some days it is peaceful and blue. Other days it is full of buoys, rocks, jellyfish, floating beach toys, confused seagulls and things that probably shouldn’t be floating there at all.

The farther Joe paddles, the stranger the ocean becomes.

Coffee Mode

Coffee is Joe’s greatest weakness. And possibly his greatest power. Collect enough coffee and Joe enters COFFEE MODE. His eyes open wide. His paddle goes crazy. The world explodes into colour. Everything moves faster. Much faster.

For a few glorious seconds, Ananas Joe becomes the fastest pineapple on the ocean. Then the caffeine wears off. And Joe goes back to being Joe.

The Goal

There is no finish line. No final boss. No last level. Just Joe, his board and the Endless Sea.

Go farther. Score higher. Drink coffee. Try not to crash.

And maybe, somewhere out there… find the perfect wave.
